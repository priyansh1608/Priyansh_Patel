Weak Learner:
A weak learner is a model that performs only slightly better than random guessing. For example, a simple 
decision stump that checks one feature to detect whether an Instagram post may be popular.

Strong Learner:
A strong learner is a model that combines multiple weak learners to produce a much more accurate prediction. 
For example, a boosting model could combine many small decision trees to detect suspicious transactions in a 
Paytm-like payment system.

Main Difference:
A weak learner has limited predictive power, while a strong learner provides much better predictions, often 
by combining several weak learners.
