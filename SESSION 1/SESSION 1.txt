Q - 2 : Given the following scenarios, classify each as supervised, unsupervised, or reinforcement learning:
        1) Netflix recommending movies based on your watch history, 
        2) Spotify grouping similar songs into playlists, 
        3) A self-driving car learning to park by trial and error. Write your answers with a one-line 
           explanation for each choice.


Answer : 


Netflix recommending movies based on your watch history — Unsupervised Learning
It finds patterns in your viewing behavior and recommends movies that are similar to your interests.

Spotify grouping similar songs into playlists — Unsupervised Learning
It groups songs with similar characteristics without requiring predefined labels.

A self-driving car learning to park by trial and error — Reinforcement Learning
The car learns by taking actions and receiving rewards or penalties based on the result.

Q - 4 : Imagine you are building a spam filter for WhatsApp messages. Write a short paragraph explaining how
        traditional programming would approach this problem versus how machine learning would approach it.
        <br><br><em><strong>Hint:</strong> Focus on rules vs learning from data.</em>



Answer : 



In traditional programming, a spam filter would use manually created rules, such as blocking messages 
containing certain words, links, or suspicious patterns. These rules must be written and updated by the
programmer. In machine learning, the spam filter is trained using a dataset of messages labeled as spam
or not spam. The ML model learns patterns from the data and uses those patterns to classify new WhatsApp 
messages automatically.
