Q - 5 : Give a real example (outside of healthcare/hospital) of overfitting from any app you use 
        (e.g., Instagram, Flipkart, Swiggy), and explain in 2-3 lines why it might happen in that scenario.



Answer : 

Example: Instagram Feed Recommendation

Instagram's recommendation system may overfit if it learns too strongly from a user's recent likes, 
clicks, or interactions. For example, if a user watches many cricket videos for a short period, an 
overfitted model might keep recommending mostly cricket content and fail to recognize the user's other 
interests. This can happen when the model becomes too sensitive to limited or temporary user activity.
