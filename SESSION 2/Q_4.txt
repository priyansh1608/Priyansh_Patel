Q - 4 : Draw or find a graph online that visually explains the bias–variance tradeoff, and write a short note
       describing how this tradeoff would affect predictions in a Zomato restaurant rating predictor app.



Answer : 

The bias–variance tradeoff explains the balance between a model being too simple and too complex. 
A simple model can have high bias and underfit the data, while a complex model can have high variance 
and overfit the training data.

In a Zomato restaurant rating predictor, high bias may cause the model to give similar and inaccurate 
ratings to different restaurants. High variance may cause the model to memorize the training restaurants 
and give poor predictions for new restaurants. Therefore, a balanced model should be selected so that it 
gives accurate predictions on unseen restaurant data.
